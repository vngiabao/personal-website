## Product Purpose

Bao Vo's personal portfolio connects engineering, strategy, and entrepreneurship through real projects and a personal chronological narrative. Existing site language: “From silicon to strategy.”

## Positioning

The portfolio documents a foundation in materials and circuit design, commercialization work, and co-founding A Story. Case studies, photographs, the interactive Book, and the career chronology provide the evidence.

## Users

Inferred from the portfolio content: prospective collaborators, employers, and people interested in Bao's ventures and work. No narrower primary audience has been confirmed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing Next.js web application. Preserve factual copy, navigation, photos, project evidence, responsive behavior, and accessible home links while replacing the personal logo and icon.

## Brand Commitments

The user rejected the BV monogram and asked for a luxury, premium identity that fits the overall site and personal values. They explicitly delegated creative direction with no requirement to use initials. The existing navy, cream, and brass editorial site supplies the context; branding must feel integrated with it.

The user also rejected the subsequent open-folio mark as weird; neither concept is an approved identity. Their informal nickname is GB Virtuoso, used in connection with card magic and cardistry. They love the yellow Virtuoso deck and associate Virtuoso with mastery, with a personal connection between Virtuoso and Vo. Their intended professional fields are chips, entrepreneurship, and consulting. These are optional creative inputs, not a requirement to depict every interest or rename the professional site. Their supplied real signature is a separate personal asset, not approved as the primary logo.

Latest correction: the user rejected the cardistry-inspired abstract concept. Focus subsequent identity exploration on the letters and name **GB Virtuoso** and the professional purpose. Do not use cardistry as the design story or imagery. The latest typography-only concept is a proposal, not yet approved for implementation.
